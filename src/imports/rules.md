## Game Analysis Summary
Rapid Rumble is a fast-paced category word game for 3-6 players, ages 6+.
- Time: 15-30 minutes
- Goal: Be first to discard all 5 letter cards
- Components: 120 Letter Cards (A-Z), 50 Category Cards, Instructions
- Setup: Deal 5 letter cards to each player
- Gameplay: Category Caller draws a category card; players shout words that fit the category AND start with one of their letters
- Players discard one card per round when they give a valid answer
- Role of Category Caller rotates each round

## How to Play

### Setup
1. Shuffle the Letter Cards and deal 5 cards face down to each player.
2. Each player places their letter cards in front of them, letter side up.
3. Shuffle the Category Cards and place the deck in the center of the table.
4. Choose a player to be the first Category Caller. This role rotates each round.

### Gameplay
1. Flip the Top Category Card
   The Category Caller draws and reads the top Category Card aloud (e.g., "Things in the Kitchen").

2. Players Respond Rapid
   All other players must quickly call out a word that:
   - Matches the category
   - Starts with one of the letters in front of them
   Example: If the category is "Things in the Kitchen" and your cards are B, D, L, M, and T, you could shout:
   - "Bowl"
   - "Ladle"
   - "Toaster"

   The first correct answer for a letter lets the player discard that Letter Card. Players can only discard one card per round.

3. Check and Confirm
   Once a player shouts a valid answer:
   - The group agrees it fits the category.
   - The player discards that letter card face down.
   If multiple players speak at the same time, only the first valid answer counts.
   If a player gives an invalid answer (wrong category or wrong letter), they keep all their cards and skip that round.

4. New Round
   Pass the role of Category Caller clockwise.
   Play another round by drawing a new Category Card.

### Winning the Game
The first player to discard all 5 Letter Cards is the winner.

### Variations & Tips
* Play with more cards: For longer games, deal 7–10 letters instead of 5.
* Younger players: Allow more time or make it cooperative by helping each other come up with words.
* Tiebreakers: If two players finish in the same round, have a final showdown round with one Letter Card each.
